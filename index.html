The question

What if the restaurant opening felt more like a fashion campaign than a restaurant commercial?

Food, clothing, performance, location and filmmaking. One world.

The lens

Fashion is how we enter.

Rather than a restaurant commercial, the opening borrows the visual language of Shanghai Tang fashion. The clothes come first. The restaurant is discovered through them.
