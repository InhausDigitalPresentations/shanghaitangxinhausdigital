01 / 06The idea

Our Food Is the Story

At most cinemas, food plays a supporting role.

At Cinemacity, it becomes the main character.

We meet a chef who speaks about food as though he were a celebrated film director discussing his latest work. Through his eyes, every cuisine becomes a different kind of cinema — dramatic, provocative or larger than life.

As the interview becomes increasingly intimate and cinematic, we discover one simple truth:

Here, food doesn’t play a supporting role.

Our food is the story.

The campaign’s first symbolic device — it introduces the chef-as-director before he appears.

  • FormatCinema pre-roll
  • Length35 seconds
  • BridgeAt the cinema. At home.

Performance and pacing

The pause after “Here…” matters. The moment should feel conspiratorial rather than staged. Let the chef move toward the camera instead of delivering a conventional brand line from his chair. The campaign line is the resolution, not an extra slogan appended afterward.

05 / 06Art direction

Art direction

The actor, the camera and the food all share one authored visual world.

The chef as auteur

Cast an actor, not one of Cinemacity’s working chefs. He should feel like a creative genius: part chef, part film director and part artist. He is eccentric and expressive, but never comic or theatrical for its own sake.

  • A chef’s jacket worn slightly open
  • Kitchen tongs tucked into the jacket pocket like a director’s pen
  • Hair that looks messy, but deliberately arranged
  • Artist’s trousers carrying subtle, intentional traces of paint
  • Expressive hands, quiet confidence and controlled eccentricity
  • Paint marks must read as artistic, never unhygienic; the jacket stays pristine enough to preserve food quality cues

A camera that becomes curious

  1. 01Composed interview
  2. 02Closer framing
  3. 03Dutch or low angle
  4. 04Intimate confession

The camera is not simply recording him. It becomes increasingly fascinated by him until the lens feels like his confidant. Each return to the interview must visibly advance this relationship.

Food as the star

  • Avoid predictable menu photography and passive beauty shots
  • Show ingredients in extreme macro: texture, freshness, steam, flame and movement
  • Choose the single most expressive action for each dish
  • Use precise, sculptural lighting that makes familiar food feel monumental
  • The images must communicate restaurant-grade preparation rather than standard cinema concessions

06 / 06One production, every screen

One production,
every screen

How the idea becomes a flexible campaign system.

01

Cinema pre-roll

The complete 35-second story, designed for the scale, sound and visual impact of the cinema screen.

02

Social awareness

Short cuisine-led edits in vertical and square formats. Each version can focus on a different cinematic genre, with a direct dine-in or delivery message.

03

Organic social

Macro food sequences, individual dish stories, character moments with the chef and behind-the-scenes content that extends the campaign world.

04

Interior corridor screens

Soundless food films built around expressive action shots, bold typography and the campaign line. Spoken interventions are replaced by graphic statements.

Dramatic.Spicy.Larger than life.Our food is the story.

This is not only one pre-roll film. It is a recognisable content system that can move from a cinema screen to paid social, organic content and silent interior displays without losing the central idea.
